# Sequential suggestions qualify under explicit duplicate policies

The fixed [qualification declaration](suggestion-sequence-qualification.md)
passed its component, durable-policy, restart, accounting and independent
numerical checks. All **84 component calls returned**. The durable campaigns
consumed **142 of 160 planned analytical slots**, rejected six duplicate
suggestions, and retained 12 later slots as unknown. All eight replicate-policy
campaigns completed their ten-evaluation budgets. Correct rejection stopped
six reject-policy campaigns without analytical execution or spend for the
rejected candidate.

These are deliberately constructed deterministic fixtures. Qualification
establishes operation of the declared scalar Gaussian-regression route and
service contracts on these cases. It does not estimate acceptance probability,
compare optimizer performance, validate physical retention parameters, or
establish manuscript parity. The original
[543-observation study](synthetic-ablation-results.md), including its 57 unknown
slots and unavailable rejected-call timing, remains unchanged.

## Replay and spend are checked against persisted evidence

The independent audit verifies 148 immutable suggestion-attempt records: 142
accepted records and six rejected records. It checks original-key replay for
all 142 admitted suggestions and six rejection outcomes, and replay of all
142 consumptions. Restart evidence covers all 48 reached worker chunks and
32 preceding-receipt replays. Each process resumes by reading existing
receipts; replay does not dispatch a new suggestion or charge another
evaluation.

There are 142 completed simulator attempts, 142 analytical observations and
142 consumed evaluations, with no completed attempt missing from consumption.
The declared cost is **142 synthetic evaluation units**. The 18 repeated
candidates retain fresh method, proposal, experiment, attempt, prediction,
Gaussian result, observation, snapshot and consumption identities. Repeated
coordinates remain in ordered training. Repetition in this noiseless simulator
does not create independent chemical samples or measured noise.

The full cohort has 320 planned analyte slots: 284 executed analyte cases and
36 unavailable cases associated with rejected or unattempted slots. Censoring
stays analytically unknown even when its confirmation reward is zero.

expected values. It recomputes retention, censoring, Gaussian resolution,
three-valued acceptance, historical utility, confirmation reward and resources
with the frozen 70-digit Decimal oracle. It checks all 142 predictions,
142 Gaussian runs, 142 consumptions, 284 analyte prediction rows and 568 raw
metric rows. There are no numerical disagreements or threshold-ambiguous
cases. It also verifies all 160 CSV rows, 2,242 source-identity nodes, ordered
training, owner/foreign-key closure and actual duplicate coverage under each
prior. The independently derived qualification agrees with the recorded result.

The [supplemental audit](reference-data/suggestion-sequences/independent-review-supplement.json)
records 2,188 finite numerical comparisons across 426 stage records. Maximum
absolute errors are 1.48e-16 min for retention, 3.39e-15 for dimensionless
resolution and 4.28e-15 for utility. These are arithmetic-agreement errors,
not estimates of model or physical measurement uncertainty.

## Inspect and reproduce

- [All 160 durable slots](reference-data/suggestion-sequences/slots.csv), keyed
  by `case_id` and zero-based `ordinal`, retain exact sealed export bytes.
- [Recorded criteria and counts](reference-data/suggestion-sequences/report.json)
  include all 12 component-case summaries and the 16 campaign criteria.
- [Publication receipt](reference-data/suggestion-sequences/publication.json)
  binds these copies and the independent audit to the frozen declaration,
  pre-call manifest, executed build and full-evidence seal.

The sealed qualification bundle retains raw requests/results, all journals,
worker receipts, complete campaign evidence graphs, the SQLite backup and the
frozen numerical oracle. The separate delivery archive also retains the
independent auditor sources, tests and audit outputs. A completion seal means
publication finished; the
separate criteria and audit establish qualification. The compact reference
above is not a substitute for the full raw bundle when auditing execution.

From a source checkout or extracted source archive, configure the optional
backend and run the [installed qualification command](suggestion-sequence-qualification.md#run-the-installed-distribution)
with new data and output directories. Then render the sealed bundle:

```sh
Rscript scripts/plot_suggestion_evidence_allocation.R \
  /tmp/measure-suggestion-sequence-output /tmp/measure-suggestion-sequence-figures
```

The plotter needs `ggplot2`, `jsonlite` and `digest`. It verifies sealed input
hashes and complete slot grids, keeps outputs separate, and refuses nonempty
output directories. The published PNG and SVG show the same verified values.
New executions have new IDs and timestamps; compare logical keys and recorded
software/settings before interpreting candidate differences.

A fresh optimization comparison now needs its own prospective declaration.

filtering, fitted retention models, physical measurements and multi-fidelity
campaigns retain separate requirements in
[#41](https://github.com/JamesHWade/measure-service/issues/41) and the broader
benchmark backlog.
