# Qualify sequential suggestions before a new comparison

The frozen `measure-service-suggestion-sequence-qualification/1` declaration
tests the public BO-MCP scalar Gaussian-regression route, durable suggestion
evidence and the explicit duplicate policies in
[ablation/2](campaign-replication.md). It is a software and analytical-contract
qualification with deliberately designed fixtures. It does not estimate which
optimizer is better or reproduce the manuscript's task cohort.

The declaration bytes have SHA256
`d85f83d19076e266e4e159253d35e7baa9baea2cde32db225b1be9d7bfc4f4ef`.
The initial coordinate streams and 70-digit Decimal references were checked
before any qualification optimizer call. The same four logical task IDs appear
in every protocol; only their registered opaque producer and contract IDs are
rebound. Target, noise prior and duplicate policy do not change the common
initial coordinates.

| Part | Fixed design | Planned denominator |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| Component sequences | 2 targets × 2 noise priors × all-zero, all-one and mixed fabricated histories; seven sequential calls per sequence | 84 backend calls |
| Durable campaigns | 4 designed analytical fixtures × 2 priors × reject/replicate; 10 evaluations including 3 initial points | 160 analytical slots |
| Analytes | 2 required analytes in every analytical slot | 320 analyte slots |

Component histories begin with a repeated coordinate and retain every row.
Each returned candidate receives the next fixed fabricated score. These rows
stress numerical readiness and never become analytical observations or spend.
A failed sequence retains the failing call and its future unknown slots; it
does not switch optimizer or retry fitting.

The four durable fixtures are accepted throughout the domain, not accepted
throughout the domain, censored throughout the domain, and mixed at the three
initial points. They use declared linear retention laws and Gaussian widths.
Censoring remains analytically unknown even when its confirmation reward is
zero. Every replicate has fresh execution evidence and charged spend. Equal
inputs in this deterministic simulator are not independent noisy measurements.

## Run the installed distribution

Configure the [optional BO runtime](campaign-runtime.md#optional-real-bo-mcp-runtime)
and install the same service wheel in the host and sidecar environments. Run
from outside the checkout with a new empty data directory and a new output:

```sh
python -m measure_service.benchmarks qualify-suggestion-sequences \
  --data-dir /tmp/measure-suggestion-sequence-store \
  --output-dir /tmp/measure-suggestion-sequence-output
```

The runner freezes installed source identities, actual distribution versions,
effective acquisition settings and the declaration before its first backend
call. It records configured null acquisition overrides separately from the
backend's positive integer defaults. It checks that the selected implementation
stays the same throughout the run. These hashes record the executed software;
they are not dependency revision restrictions.

Durable campaigns run in fresh processes for ordinals 0–2, 3–6 and 7–9. A new
process reads and replays the preceding immutable receipt without dispatching
another suggestion. Rejected or failed keys replay their original outcomes.
Committed records survive a lost worker response; unavailable response or
journal timing remains unavailable. A recovered database record does not turn
a failed operational check into a successful qualification.

## Read each criterion separately

Full qualification requires sequential numerical readiness, durable policy
compliance, completion of all eight replicate campaigns, actual duplicate
admission and rejection under each prior, and independent numerical and
identity validation. Reject-mode budget completion is reported separately: a
correct duplicate rejection can stop that campaign while satisfying its policy.
An unexercised branch is `not_exercised`, rather than a pass. Cases and settings
are never changed after outcomes to force coverage.

The output retains all planned slots, exact ordered requests, available returned
candidates, warnings and failures, raw service responses, the SQLite evidence
backup, retry/restart checks, resource accounting, CSV projections and a seal.
Host invocation time, child-process time and declared analytical acquisition
duration have different boundaries. Missing time is null. Completed simulator
attempt spend remains separate from consumed training rows, including when a
later assessment or delivery step fails. A complete seal means the artifact is
fully published; it does not mean every qualification criterion passed.

An independent audit recomputes retention, detector censoring, Gaussian
resolution, three-valued acceptance, confirmation reward, historical utility
and declared resources from the exact binary64 inputs using 70-digit Decimal
arithmetic. It checks the full two-analyte cohort, immutable source graph,
ordered training and actual SQLite records. Threshold decisions remain exact;
comparison tolerances do not relax acceptance rules.

Keep the original [543-observation reference](synthetic-ablation-results.md),
its 57 unknown slots and unavailable rejected-call timing unchanged. A later
optimization comparison needs its own prospective declaration after this
qualification is accepted. Compoundwise surrogates, alternative covariance or
classification models, manuscript cohort selection and physical instrument
validation retain separate requirements.
